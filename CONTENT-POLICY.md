# Content policy

This repository is public. Two categories of material must never enter it.

## No third-party musical content

No score files, MIDI, audio, or transcriptions of music the repository
owner did not write. This includes material **derived** from a collection of
such files: extracted vocabularies, statistical priors, manifests, fingerprints
and calibration snapshots.

Tests must not depend on any file at all. `src/fixtures.rs` generates the
documents they need from generic instrumental material — scales, repeated
frets, power chords, note values, standard drum patterns. Nothing transcribed
from a recording.

## No copyrighted reference texts

No full or partial copies of dissertations, books, or paid lesson material, and
no quotations beyond ordinary citation. Cite by author, year and page.

## No private project detail

No unreleased song or album titles, no private repository or host names, and no
narration of internal work history.
