# Content policy

This repository is public. Third-party material without a licence that allows
redistribution must never enter it.

## No third-party musical content

No score files, MIDI, audio, or transcriptions of music written by someone
else, and nothing derived from such material: no vocabularies, priors,
manifests or calibration snapshots measured from it.

Tests must not depend on any file at all. `src/fixtures.rs` generates the
documents they need from generic instrumental material — scales, repeated
frets, power chords, note values, standard drum patterns. Nothing transcribed
from a recording.

## No copyrighted reference texts

No full or partial copies of dissertations, books, papers, or paid lesson
material, and no quotations beyond ordinary citation. Cite by author, year and
page.

## No private detail

No unreleased song or album titles, no private repository or host names, no
credentials, and no narration of internal work history.
